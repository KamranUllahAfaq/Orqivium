# Legal and Compatibility Records

This directory isolates source-provenance, third-party authorship, and required
compatibility notices from the Orqivium product identity.

- `SOURCE_PROVENANCE.md` records the inspected source baseline.
- `THIRD_PARTY_AUTHORS` preserves contributor records supplied with that source.
- `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` preserves notices that accompanied the source.
- The repository-root `LICENSE` remains the controlling distribution license.

Orqivium product documentation, user interfaces, release metadata, and
maintainer records identify Kamran Ullah Afaq as the builder and maintainer of
Orqivium-specific work.
